# Sales — AI prompts

Copy any prompt below into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, Perplexity or Grok. Replace the `{{ }}` parts and “(paste here)” with your own details.

## Follow-up after a call

```
Write a follow-up email to send after a sales call.
- Friendly but professional, under 150 words
- Recap the two most important things we discussed
- End with one clear next step and a date
- No pushy language

Call notes:
(paste your notes here)
```

## Cold outreach opener

```
Write a short cold email to someone I haven't spoken to before.
- Give me 3 subject lines
- Open with one specific reason I'm writing to them (not a generic compliment)
- One sentence on what's in it for them
- Ask for a 15-minute call, and make it easy to say no
- Under 120 words

About the person and my offer:
(paste here)
```

```
1. Reframe around value
2. Trade something (scope, term, timing) for the price
3. Politely hold the line
For each, write the exact reply and when it works best.

```

```
(paste here)
```

```
Our product:
(paste here)

```

## Re-engaging a cold lead

```
Write a short email to reopen a conversation with a lead who went quiet.
- Acknowledge the silence lightly, no guilt-tripping
- Offer one useful thing (an idea, a resource, a data point)
- Ask a yes/no question that's easy to answer
- Under 100 words

History with the lead:
(paste here)
```
