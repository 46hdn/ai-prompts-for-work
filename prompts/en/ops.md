# Operations & Admin — AI prompts

Copy any prompt below into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, Perplexity or Grok. Replace the `{{ }}` parts and “(paste here)” with your own details.

## Professional email draft

```
Write a professional email for the situation below.
Give a subject line and a body under 150 words. If it's a request, state what I need and by when; if it's a decline, keep it kind but clear.

Situation and recipient:
(paste here)
```

## Apology email

```
Write an apology email for the situation below.
Apologize first, explain what happened without excuses, say what we're doing about it and how we'll prevent a repeat.
Stick to facts; don't over-admit liability.

Situation:
(paste here)
```

## Announcement to the team

```
Write an internal announcement about {{Subject}}.
It takes effect {{Date}} and affects {{Who}}.
Cover what's changing, who needs to do what, and where to ask questions. Tone: {{Tone:Friendly|Neutral|Formal}}.
```

## Scheduling email

```
Write an email to schedule a meeting.
Offer three time slots, state the duration and whether it's in person or online, and make replying as easy as picking one.

Who and what for (with candidate times):
(paste here)
```

## Turn notes into an SOP

```
Turn my notes into a standard operating procedure someone new could follow.
Sections: purpose, what you need, numbered steps (one action each), common mistakes, who to contact.

Notes:
(paste here)
```

## Summarize a long thread

```
Summarize the email thread below for someone who wasn't in it.
Three-line summary, decisions made, open questions, what each person needs to do next.
Don't add anything that isn't in the thread.

Thread:
(paste here)
```

## Chasing a late payment or reply

```
Write a polite follow-up to someone who hasn't replied or paid.
Reference the earlier message, restate what I need and the new date, and keep it non-accusatory.
Give me a gentle version and a firmer version.

Background:
(paste here)
```
